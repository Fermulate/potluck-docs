Your friends are the people you share a group with. Nobody sends friend requests: when someone joins one of your groups, you show up on each other's list.

Open **Groups** and tap **Friends** to see them. Each row shows where you stand with that person across every group you share, your one-on-one tab included: **owes you**, **you owe**, **settled up**, or **no tab yet**.

## What the totals mean

A friend's total adds up what the two of you owe each other in every shared group.

- **Groups in the same currency are added up.** If you owe Kai $20 in one group and Kai owes you $15 in another, the row says **you owe** $5.00.
- **Currencies are never converted.** Each currency gets its own amount, joined with "and", like **you owe** $20.00 and €8.00.
- **Each direction gets its own line.** If you owe Kai dollars but Kai owes you euros, the row shows both: **you owe** $20.00 on one line and **owes you** €15.00 on the next. One never hides the other.
- **Archived groups count too.** Their balances stay, so they are part of the total.

When you have a balance with someone, a line under their name says how many groups it is in, like **in 1 group** or **across 3 groups**, with a **Settle up** button. If all of those groups are archived, there is no button.

## Settle up with a friend

**Settle up** on a friend's row settles every group you share with them in one go.

<Steps>

1. Tap **Settle up** under the friend's name. The sheet is titled **Settle up with** their name.
2. Each group with a balance is listed and ticked, with the full amount and whether you owe or they owe you. Untick a group to leave it, or lower an amount to pay part of it.
3. Check **Paid on**, then tap **Record** with the number of payments.

</Steps>

Potluck records one payment in each group you picked, in that group's currency. Archived groups are shown faded and stay as they are. For the details, see [Settle up with a friend across groups](/balances/settle-up/#settle-up-with-a-friend-across-groups).

## Split something with one friend

Not everything belongs in a group. Coffee with a roommate, a concert ticket for one friend: keep those on a one-on-one tab.

<Steps>

1. On **Friends**, tap the person.
2. Potluck opens your tab together, titled **You and** their name. The first time, it starts a fresh tab.
3. Tap **Add expense** and split it as usual. See [Adding an expense](/expenses/).

</Steps>

A one-on-one tab has only **Expenses** and **Balances**. There are no plans, invite links or roles to manage. You settle up the same way as in a group: see [Recording a payment](/balances/settle-up/).

:::note[Good to know]
A one-on-one tab is separate from the groups you share. Money you owe someone in a group stays in that group, and money on your one-on-one tab stays there. The total on **Friends** adds them up, and [Settle up with a friend](#settle-up-with-a-friend) pays each one separately.
:::

## Add a friend

Tap **Add a friend** and pick one of your groups under **Invite them to**. Potluck makes a one-use invite link for that group that lasts a week, and opens your share sheet so you can send it. When they join, they appear on your Friends list.

No groups yet? Potluck asks you to start one first. See [Groups](/groups/).
