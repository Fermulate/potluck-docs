## Is Potluck free?

Yes. No subscription, no ads, no premium tier. Potluck is free software under the [AGPL-3.0](https://github.com/Fermulate/potluck/blob/main/LICENSE) license, and its code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Fermulate/potluck).

## Does Potluck move money?

No. Potluck keeps track of who owes whom. You pay each other however you like (cash, bank transfer, a payment app) and then [record the payment](/balances/settle-up/).

## Do my friends need an account?

Not to start. You can [add someone by name](/groups/#people-not-on-potluck-yet) and split with them right away. To see the group and settle up themselves, they make an account, which takes a minute. When they do, everything split for them becomes theirs and the balances stay the same.

## Is there an app in the store?

Not yet. Potluck is a web app you can [install to your home screen](/getting-started/#install-potluck-as-an-app). It then opens like any other app and can send push notifications.

## Can I use Potluck just for planning, or just for money?

Yes. Groups have both, but nothing makes you use either. Plenty of groups only ever split bills.

## Can I split something with one person, outside a group?

Yes. Open **Friends**, tap the person, then tap **Add expense** to start a one-on-one tab. See [Split something with one friend](/friends/#split-something-with-one-friend).

## Why does one person pay a cent more?

Because amounts like $100 split three ways do not divide into whole cents. Potluck hands out the leftover cents one at a time so the total is exact, and nobody pays more than one cent over their share. See [How rounding works](/expenses/splits/#how-rounding-works).

## Why does Potluck say I owe someone I never split anything with?

That is [simplified debts](/balances/#simplified-debts) at work. It cuts the number of payments in a group without changing what anyone owes in total. An owner or admin can turn it off in [Group settings](/groups/settings/#house-rules).

## Can I change a group's currency?

Only before any money is added. After that it is locked, because changing it would change what everyone owes. You can still add expenses in [any currency](/expenses/currencies/).

## Can I leave a group while I owe money?

No. Settle up first, so nobody is left with a debt they cannot collect. See [the open balance rule](/groups/roles/#the-open-balance-rule).

## Who can see my expenses?

Only the people in the group they belong to. See [Privacy and security](/privacy-and-security/).

## Can I undo a deleted expense?

Yes. Tap **Undo** right after deleting, or open the expense later and tap **Restore**. See [Editing and deleting](/expenses/editing/#undo-and-restore).

## Can I export my data?

Each group's **Expenses** tab has an **Export CSV** button that downloads every expense in the group. See [Groups](/groups/#download-a-groups-expenses).

## Does Potluck work offline?

Not really. Potluck needs a connection to load and save your groups. If you lose signal, it says **You seem to be offline. We will retry when you are back.** Times you paint on a plan keep retrying on their own.

## Something is wrong or missing. Where do I say so?

Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Fermulate/potluck/issues). Check [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/) first, the answer might be there.
