An expense says three things: how much, who paid, and who it was for. Potluck works out what everyone owes from there.

## Add an expense

<Steps>

1. In a group, tap **Add expense** on the **Expenses** tab. From anywhere else, tap the big plus button, then **Add expense**, and pick the group under **Which group is this for?**

2. Type the **Amount**. The yellow chip next to it shows the currency. Tap it to pay in a different one; see [Currencies](/expenses/currencies/).

   <Screenshot
     name="expense-new"
     alt="The New expense screen. A large amount field reads 0 next to a yellow USD chip. Below are the What was it? field with the hint Tacos, gas, the cabin, a set of category chips from Food to Other, a Paid by menu set to You, a Date field, and a red Save expense button."
   />

3. Fill in **What was it?**. Potluck usually suggests a **Category** from what you type. Keep it, or tap another one. See [Categories](#categories).

4. Check **Paid by** (you, unless you change it) and the **Date**. If more than one person paid, see [Several people paid](#several-people-paid).

5. Under **Split between**, choose who it was for and how to split it. **Equal** with everyone ticked is the default, unless your group has saved a [default split](/expenses/splits/#default-split). The six ways to split are explained in [Splitting](/expenses/splits/).

6. Add **Notes** or a **Receipt** if you like, and choose whether it **Repeats** (see [Repeating expenses](/expenses/recurring/)).

7. Tap **Save expense**. Potluck confirms what you added and what your share is.

</Steps>

:::note[Good to know]
You can add an expense that someone else paid. Pick them under **Paid by**. Anyone in the group can add expenses, and the person who added it or anyone who paid it can change it later. See [Editing and deleting](/expenses/editing/).
:::

## Categories

A category says what kind of expense it was. It powers the [category filter](#filter-the-list) and the **By category** chart on [Totals](/expenses/search-and-totals/#totals-and-charts). It is optional. When you filter, an expense without one counts as **Other**.

There are 16 categories: Food, Groceries, Drinks, Transport, Gas, Lodging, Tickets, Fun, Rent, Utilities, Home, Shopping, Gifts, Health, Life and Other.

### Subcategories

Want to be more exact? Pick a category and a second row, **More exact**, opens under it with its subcategories. Tap one, like **Taxi** under Transport.

| Category | Subcategories |
| --- | --- |
| **Food** | Eating out, Takeout, Coffee, Snacks |
| **Drinks** | Bar, Wine and spirits |
| **Transport** | Taxi, Train and bus, Flights, Car rental, Parking, Tolls, Bike, Ferry |
| **Gas** | Charging |
| **Lodging** | Hotel, Holiday rental, Camping |
| **Fun** | Movies, Concerts, Games, Sports |
| **Rent** | Mortgage |
| **Utilities** | Electricity, Heating, Water, Internet, Phone, Trash, Cleaning |
| **Home** | Furniture, Household supplies, Repairs, Electronics, Pets, Services |
| **Shopping** | Clothes |
| **Health** | Doctor, Pharmacy, Fitness |
| **Life** | Childcare, Education, Insurance, Taxes |

Groceries, Tickets, Gifts and Other have no subcategories.

- Tap the picked subcategory again to go back to just its category.
- Tap the picked category again to clear the category.

Subcategories always count toward their category:

- **Totals** add them up under their category. A taxi counts as Transport in the **By category** chart.
- **Filtering** by a category includes its subcategories. Filter by Transport and taxis and flights show up too. Filter by Taxi and you see only taxis.
- **Spreadsheets** name a subcategory by its own name, like Taxi, and an [import](/groups/settings/#import-expenses) reads it back.

### Suggested categories

As you type **What was it?**, Potluck picks a category for you and marks it **Suggested**. It looks in three places, in this order:

1. **This group's own expenses.** If the same description was used here before, it suggests the category that expense had, newest first. So if your group files "Costco" under Home, so does Potluck.
2. **A list of common words**, in every language Potluck speaks. "Uber" is Taxi, "Starbucks" is Coffee, "Airbnb" is Holiday rental. The longest match wins, so "Uber Eats" is Takeout, not Taxi.
3. **A word you used before.** If a longer word in your description (four letters or more) appeared in an earlier description in this group, it suggests that expense's category.

The suggestion follows what you type until you tap a category yourself. After that, Potluck leaves your choice alone. If nothing fits, no category is picked.

Editing an expense keeps the category it was saved with. Potluck only suggests while you add a new one.

## Several people paid

Sometimes the bill gets paid in pieces. Ava put $60 on her card and Ben paid the other $30 in cash. Record it as one expense with two payers.

<Steps>

1. Type the **Amount** first, so Potluck knows the total.
2. Tap **Paid by**. The **Who paid?** sheet opens.
3. Switch from **One person** to **Several people**.
4. Enter what each person paid. Leave anyone who paid nothing empty.
5. Tap **Done**.

</Steps>

The amounts must add up to the total. The bottom of the sheet keeps count as you type: **$30.00 left to assign**, **$5.00 too much**, or **Everything adds up**. If something is still left, a **Rest to** button (for example **Rest to Ben**) gives the whole remainder to the first person with nothing typed yet. You cannot save until it adds up.

> A $90 dinner split equally between Ava, Ben and Cam, so $30 each. Ava paid $60 and Ben paid $30. Ava is owed $30, Ben is square, and Cam owes Ava $30.

On the expense and in the list, every payer is named, for example **Ava and Ben paid $90.00**. To go back to a single payer, open **Paid by** again and pick **One person**.

## Refunds

Money that comes back is a refund: a returned jacket, a cancelled booking, a deposit you got back. Record it so it lowers what people owe.

<Steps>

1. Add an expense as usual, with the amount that came back.
2. Turn on **This is a refund**, just under **Paid by**.
3. **Paid by** becomes **Received by**. Pick whoever got the money. Several people can share it, the same way as [several payers](#several-people-paid).
4. Under **Split between**, pick who the money belongs to, and split it any way you like.
5. Tap **Save expense**.

</Steps>

A refund works like an expense in reverse. Whoever got the money owes everyone in the split their part of it.

> Ava paid $120 for four concert tickets, split equally between Ava, Ben, Cam and Dev. One show was moved and the venue sent Ava $40 back. Record a $40 refund, received by Ava, split equally between the same four. Ben, Cam and Dev each owe $10 less, and Ava is owed $30 less.

Refunds carry a **Refund** badge in the list. Your line reads **you get back** when someone else got the money, or **you owe** when you did. A refund cannot repeat, so **Repeats** is hidden while the switch is on.

## Quick split for two

When a group or one-on-one tab has just two people, four buttons save you a few taps:

- **You paid, split equally**
- **Ben owes you all of it**
- **Ben paid, split equally**
- **You owe Ben all of it**

Each one sets **Paid by** and the split in one tap: the **split equally** buttons halve it, and the **all of it** buttons put the whole amount on the other person. On a phone they sit right under **Paid by**. On a wider screen they are at the top of **Split between**, under **Quick split**. Anything else, like percentages or exact amounts, is still there in the full split options below.

## Reading the expense list

The **Expenses** tab lists expenses and payments by day, newest first. Each line tells you where it leaves you:

- **you lent** an amount: you paid, and others owe you their share.
- **you borrowed** an amount: someone else paid, and this is your share.
- **not involved**: you were not part of the split.

[Refunds](#refunds) read the other way round: **you get back** or **you owe**.

Payments between people show as, for example, **Noor paid you $35.10**. Use **Search expenses** to find something in this group, or tap a category chip to see only that kind of expense. To search every group at once, see [Search everything](/expenses/search-and-totals/#search-everything).

## Filter the list

Tap the filters button next to **Search expenses** to narrow the **Expenses** tab down. Pick as many as you like:

- **When**: **Any time**, **This month**, **Last month**, **This year**, or **Pick dates** for your own **From** and **To**.
- **Who**: one person, who paid or is in the split. Pick yourself to see only what you are part of.
- **Category**: one or more of the categories used in this group. A category includes its [subcategories](#subcategories).
- **Type**: **Everything**, just **Expenses**, or just **Payments**.

The list updates as you pick. Each filter that is on shows as a chip above the list: tap a chip to remove it, or tap **Clear all** in the filters. The button shows how many filters are on.

While you filter, a line above the list adds it up, for example **12 expenses, $480.00 in all** and **Your share $120.00**. Payments have no category, so they are hidden while you filter by category.

Your filters are kept in the page address, so you can bookmark them or send the link to someone in the group. They also stay put while you move around the group: check **Balances** or **Totals**, come back to **Expenses**, and the same filters are still on.

## Expense details

Tap an expense to open it.

You see who paid (and how much each, if several people did), how it was split and what each person owes, the items of a [by-item](/expenses/splits/#by-item) bill, the receipt and notes, and the exchange rate if it was in another currency. **History** lists who added and changed it; see [Edit history](/expenses/editing/#edit-history). At the bottom, **Comments** is a place for the group to talk about this expense ("was the tip in there?").

## Expenses for a plan

When you add an expense from a plan with **Add an expense for this plan**, it is linked to that plan (**Linked to a plan**), and the plan shows what has been spent on it. See [Locking in a time](/plans/lock-in/#split-the-costs).
