Every group keeps its balances in one currency, the one chosen when the group was made. You can still add an expense in any other currency. Potluck converts it into the group's currency when you save.

## Add an expense in another currency

<Steps>

1. On **New expense**, tap the yellow currency chip next to the amount.
2. In **Pick a currency**, choose from **Other currencies**. The **Group currency** sits at the top.
3. Type the amount as it appears on the receipt. Potluck shows a preview, for example **About $156.60 in USD**, with the rate it is using and its date.
4. Save as usual. The note under the preview says it plainly: **The exact rate is locked in when you save.**

</Steps>

On the expense, both amounts appear: the original, and the converted amount with the rate.

## Your last currency is remembered

On a trip, most expenses are in the same foreign currency. So a new expense starts in the currency you used for the last expense you added in that group. Add one dinner in euros, and the next expense in that group starts in euros too.

- It is remembered per group. Your trip group can start in euros while your flat stays in dollars.
- It only picks the starting currency, while the form is still empty. If you have already typed an amount or a name, or picked a currency yourself, Potluck never switches it under you.
- Tap the chip to change it, as always. Editing an existing expense keeps that expense's own currency.

## Where the rates come from

Potluck uses the daily reference rates published by the European Central Bank, and refreshes them once a day. For each expense it takes the latest rate on or before the expense's **Date**. A dinner from last Tuesday uses last Tuesday's rate, not today's.

Once the expense is saved, its rate never changes. Editing the expense later keeps the saved rate, as long as you keep the same currency. Nobody can type in their own rate, so everyone in the group sees the same conversion.

:::note[Good to know]
Some currencies have no reference rate. They show **No rate yet** in the picker, and Potluck asks you to pick another currency. In that case, convert it yourself and enter the amount in the group currency.
:::

## Balances and payments

- Balances are always in the group's currency, and **Settle up** starts in it. You can record a payment in another currency too: it is converted at the rate for the day it was paid, like an expense. See [Paid on another day](/balances/settle-up/#paid-on-another-day).
- The group currency is **Locked once money has been added**. See [Group settings](/groups/settings/#details).
- The balance on Home adds up groups that use your **Default currency**. Groups in other currencies are listed separately as **Plus balances in** those currencies, because Potluck does not convert balances between groups.

You can change your default currency on the [Me page](/account/#preferences). It only affects new groups and the Home summary.
