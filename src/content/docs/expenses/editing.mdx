Typos happen. Here is how to fix them, and who is allowed to.

## Who can change an expense

- The person who added it.
- Anyone who paid for it, including each payer when [several people paid](/expenses/#several-people-paid).
- The group's owner and admins, for any expense.

Anyone else gets **Only the person who added or paid this, or an admin, can change it.** See [Roles and members](/groups/roles/).

## Edit an expense

<Steps>

1. Open the expense from the group's **Expenses** tab.
2. Tap **Edit**.
3. Change what you need on **Edit expense**, then tap **Save changes**.

</Steps>

Balances update straight away, and the people in the split get a notification that it changed.

## Edit history

Every expense keeps a **History** on its page. It lists who added it, who edited it, and who deleted it or brought it back, with when.

Under each edit, Potluck lists every field that changed, with the old value and the new one:

- **Amount: $90.00 to $96.00**
- **Paid by: you to you $60.00 and Ben $36.00**
- **Name**, **Currency**, **Split**, **Date**, **Category** and **Notes**
- For by-item bills: **Items**, **Tax**, **Tip** and **Discount**
- **Marked as a refund** or **No longer a refund**, **Receipt added** or **Receipt removed**, and **Linked to a different plan**

So if a number looks off, open the expense and check who changed what. Edits made before Potluck kept this detail show who edited it, without the list of changes.

:::note[Good to know]
- A [by-item](/expenses/splits/#by-item) expense opens with its items, tax, tip and discount, ready to change. One saved before Potluck kept items opens as **Exact** amounts.
- To make an expense you already added repeat, open it and tap **Make it repeat**. Repeating bills are managed under **Repeating** on the Expenses tab. See [Repeating expenses](/expenses/recurring/#make-an-expense-you-already-added-repeat).
- An expense in another currency keeps its saved exchange rate unless you change the currency.
:::

## Delete an expense

<Steps>

1. Open the expense and tap the trash button (**Delete**).
2. Confirm. Potluck reminds you: **You can undo this for a little while.**

</Steps>

The expense disappears from the list and balances update.

## Undo and restore

Right after deleting, a message says **Expense deleted.** with an **Undo** button. Tap it and the expense comes back as if nothing happened.

Missed it? Deleted expenses are not gone for good. If you open one later, for example from a notification, it says **This expense was deleted.** Tap **Restore** to bring it back, with its balances.

You cannot edit a deleted expense until you restore it. If someone in the split has left the group since, it cannot be restored.

### Recently deleted

Each group keeps a list of what was taken out of it. Open the group's **Totals** tab and tap **Recently deleted** at the bottom.

It lists deleted expenses and payments, most recently deleted first. Each one shows the amount, the date it was from, and who deleted it and when, for example **Deleted by Ben 2 days ago**. Tap one to open it.

<Steps>

1. Find the expense or payment in **Recently deleted**.
2. Tap **Restore** next to it.

</Steps>

It goes back where it was, with its balances, and Potluck says so, for example **"Taco night" is back.** Nothing deleted yet? The list says **Nothing in the bin**.

You see **Restore** on the items you are allowed to bring back:

- **Expenses**: the person who added it, anyone who paid for it, and the group's owner and admins.
- **Payments**: the person who paid, the person who was paid, whoever recorded it, and the group's owner and admins. See [Restore a deleted payment](/balances/settle-up/#restore-a-deleted-payment).

In an [archived group](#archived-groups-are-read-only), the list is there to read, but nothing can be restored.

## Archived groups are read only

When a group is [archived](/groups/settings/#archive-a-group), its expenses stay there to look at, but nobody can change them. The expense page says **Supper club is archived, so its expenses can be read but not added or changed.** (with your group's name), and there is no **Edit**, **Delete** or **Restore**, and no new comments.

To change something, an owner or admin unarchives the group first. See [Archive a group](/groups/settings/#archive-a-group).
