A receipt settles arguments before they start. Attach one to any expense.

<Steps>

1. On **New expense** (or **Edit expense**), tap **Add receipt** near the bottom of the form.
2. Take a photo or pick a file. Photos and PDFs up to 10 MB work.
3. Save the expense. The receipt uploads with it.

</Steps>

To swap it, tap **Replace**. To take it off, tap **Remove receipt**.

On the expense's page, the receipt appears under **Receipt**. Tap it to open it full size. PDFs show as **Receipt (PDF)**.

## Who can see a receipt

Receipts are private to the group. Only members of the group the expense belongs to can open them, and they are not reachable by a public link.

The person who uploaded a receipt can replace or remove it, and so can the group's owner and admins.

:::caution[Before you upload]
Photos can carry more than the receipt: card digits, an address, or the location saved by your camera. Crop the photo, or attach a screenshot of it, if that matters to you.
:::

If an upload fails, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/#a-receipt-will-not-upload).
