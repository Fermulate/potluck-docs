Rent, the internet bill, a shared streaming subscription. Set them up once and Potluck adds a copy on the day each one is due.

## Make an expense repeat

<Steps>

1. Add the expense as usual, with the first date it happened. See [Adding an expense](/expenses/).
2. Near the bottom of the form, set **Repeats** to **Every week**, **Every month** or **Every year**.
3. Tap **Save expense**.

</Steps>

The first copy appears one period after the date you entered. Each copy has the same amount, payer, split and category, and shows a small repeat icon in the list.

## See and manage repeating expenses

Everything that repeats in a group is listed under **Repeating** at the top of the **Expenses** tab, with how many are running and the next date for each.

- **Pause** stops new copies until you tap **Resume**.
- **Stop repeating** ends it for good. Expenses already added stay.

The person who set it up can pause or stop it, and so can the group's owner and admins.

:::note[Good to know]
- Monthly bills keep their day. Rent on the 31st lands on the last day of shorter months (Feb 28, Apr 30), then goes back to the 31st.
- You choose **Repeats** when you first add an expense. To change the amount or split of a repeating bill, stop it and set up a new one.
- If the payer or someone in the split leaves the group, or the group is archived, the repeating expense stops on its own.
- For a bill in another currency, each copy uses the exchange rate for its own date. See [Currencies](/expenses/currencies/).
:::
