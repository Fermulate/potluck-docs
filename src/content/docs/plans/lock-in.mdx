Once enough people have painted, someone picks the time. The plan's organizer can lock it in, and so can the group's owner and admins.

## Lock it in

<Steps>

1. Open the plan. Under **Best times**, tap **Lock it in** on the **Best bet**, or **Pick this one** on any of the **Runners up**.
2. Potluck asks **Lock in this time?** and shows the date. Tap **Lock it in**.

</Steps>

The time is stamped onto the plan and its status changes to **On the calendar**. Everyone in the group gets an **It's a date** notification.

## RSVP

Under **Are you in?**, tap **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't**. You can change your answer any time. Below the buttons, everyone's answers are grouped into **Going**, **Maybe**, **Can't make it** and **No reply**.

If the organizer set an **RSVP by** date, it shows next to the question. Home also asks **Are you in?** for plans you have not answered, with quick **Can't** and **Going** buttons.

About a day before the plan starts, people who said **Going** or **Maybe** get a **Coming up** reminder, and people who have not answered get an **Are you in?** nudge.

## Add it to your calendar

- **Add to calendar** downloads a calendar file (.ics) with the title, time, place and a link back to the plan. Open it and your calendar app adds the event. This works with Apple Calendar, Outlook and most others.
- **Google Calendar** opens Google Calendar in a new tab with the event filled in. Check it and tap **Save**.

Either way you get a copy. If the plan changes later, the copy does not update.

:::tip[Every plan, automatically]
To get every plan from every group into your calendar, and keep it up to date, subscribe to your calendar feed instead. See [Calendar feed](/account/#calendar-feed).
:::

## Split the costs

Tap **Add an expense for this plan** to record the groceries, the tickets or the cab. The expense is linked to the plan, and the plan shows the total under **On the tab**. See [Adding an expense](/expenses/).

## Call it off

<Steps>

1. Open the plan and tap **Call it off**.
2. Potluck asks **Call off this plan?** and warns that everyone will be told. Tap **Call it off**.

</Steps>

The plan is marked **Called off** and shows **This plan was called off.** Everyone in the group gets a **Plans changed** notification. Owners, admins and the organizer can call off a plan, whether it is still polling or already on the calendar.

:::caution[No take-backs]
A called-off plan cannot be reopened, and a locked-in time cannot be changed from the app. To try again, plan something new.
:::

After the plan ends, its status changes to **Done and dusted** and it moves to **Been there** on the Plans page.
