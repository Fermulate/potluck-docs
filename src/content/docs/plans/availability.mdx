When a poll opens, Potluck asks **When are you free?** You answer by painting a grid: days across the top, times down the side, in your own time zone.

## Paint your times

<Steps>

1. Open the plan from **Needs you** on Home, from **Plans**, or from the notification. Until you answer, the plan opens on **Your times**.

   <Screenshot
     name="event-poll-painter"
     alt="The Dumpling night plan, marked Finding a time. A card titled When are you free? has a Your times and Everyone switch, a brush picker with Free, Maybe and Erase, the count 0 slots painted, the hint Press and drag to paint the times that work, and an empty grid with days Friday 13 to Thursday 19 across the top and 5 PM to 9 PM down the side."
   />

2. Pick a brush:
   - **Free**: this time works for you.
   - **Maybe**: you could make it if you must.
   - **Erase**: clear cells.

3. Press and drag across the grid. Everything in the rectangle between where you started and where you are gets painted.

4. Switch brushes and paint again as needed. The counter shows how many cells you have painted.

</Steps>

There is no save button. Potluck saves as you go and shows **Saving...** and then **Saved** under the grid.

:::tip[Painting is a toggle]
If you start dragging on a cell that already has the brush's color, the drag erases instead. Tap a painted cell once to clear it.
:::

## Mouse, touch and keyboard

**Mouse or trackpad:** click and drag. One click paints one cell.

**Touch:** press and drag with one finger. While your finger is on the grid, the page does not scroll, so you can paint downwards. Scroll by dragging outside the grid.

**Keyboard:** press Tab to move into the grid, then:

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Move between cells |
| <kbd>Space</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Paint the cell with the current brush, or clear it if it already has that brush |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd> + an arrow | Paint as you go, carrying the brush into each cell you move to |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> | Leave the grid |

Screen readers announce each cell's day, time and state, for example "not set" or "Works for me".

## The live heatmap

Tap **Everyone** to see **Group availability**. Each cell gets warmer the more people can make it, from **Nobody** (pale) to **Everyone** (full tomato). A **Maybe** counts as half a person.

<Screenshot

- Hover or tap a cell to see who is free then, for example **4 of 6 free**, with **Free:** and **Maybe:** names.
- Cells you painted yourself carry a small dot.
- The grid updates live. When a friend paints, their cells warm up while you watch.

When you are on **Everyone** and have not answered yet, a reminder appears with an **Add your times** button.

## Guest links

Some people you want at the table are not in the group, or not on Potluck at all. A guest link lets them add their times with just a name. No account, no app, no group invite.

### Share with guests

<Steps>

1. Open a plan that is still finding a time. Under **Guests**, tap **Share with guests**.

2. Pick how long the link stays open: **24 hours**, **7 days** or **30 days**.

3. Pick how many guests can answer through it: **5**, **10**, **25**, or **Custom** for any number from 1 to 100.

4. Tap **Create guest link**, then **Copy guest link** or the share button to send it to a group chat.

</Steps>

Anyone in the group can make a guest link. Each open link is listed on the plan with how many guests have used it, for example **3 of 10 guests**, and when it closes. A link that has run out of seats shows **Full**.

To close a link early, tap **Turn off** next to it and confirm. Whoever made the link, the organizer and group admins can turn it off. Answers guests already gave stay on the plan.

### What guests see

A guest opens the link and sees the plan's title, who invited them, the days and hours of the poll, in their own time zone. They paint their times with the same **Free**, **Maybe** and **Erase** brushes, type their name under the grid, and tap **Send my times**.

After that they see **Everyone**: how many people are free at each time, never who. It starts on the time that suits the most people; tap any other time to see its count. Guests do not see names, balances, expenses or anything else about the group.

The same browser can come back to the link and tap **Change my times** until the link closes. A guest who clears their browser data, or opens the link on another device, answers as a new guest.

A guest link stops taking answers when:

- it expires or someone turns it off,
- the plan is locked in or called off,
- the group is archived.

The guest then sees **This link has closed**, with a way to sign up for Potluck. If the link closed while they were still painting, the page also says their times were not saved. Once the plan is locked in, the link shows the day and time the group picked instead (to anyone while the link is still open, and to guests who answered after that).

When every seat is taken, new guests see **This link is full**; guests who already answered can still change their times.

### Guests on the plan

Guest answers count everywhere member answers do: in the live heatmap, in the best times, and toward the plan's minimum number of people. Their names carry a **(guest)** tag, and the **Guests** card lists everyone who answered through a link.

The organizer and group admins can remove a guest with the **X** next to their name. Their times go with them and their seat on the link opens up again, but that browser cannot answer through the link anymore.

After the plan is locked in, it keeps the list of who answered as a guest.

:::note[Good to know]
Guests are not members. A guest link never adds anyone to the group; send a [group invite](/groups/invites/) for that.
:::

## Nudge people

**Still waiting on** lists who has not added their times yet, with a count like **5 of 6 responded**. Tap **Nudge** next to a person, or **Nudge everyone**, to send them a notification. Potluck shows when you last nudged someone, and the button turns into **Nudge again**.

:::note[Good to know]
Anyone in the group can paint and nudge until the plan is locked in. After that, the grid closes and people [RSVP](/plans/lock-in/#rsvp) instead.
:::
