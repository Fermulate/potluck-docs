As people paint, Potluck ranks the possible start times under **Best times**. The top one is the **Best bet**. The next few are **Runners up**.

## The rules

**1. Everyone must be free for the whole thing.** A start time only counts you in if you painted every cell from the start to the end of the event. If dinner is 2 hours and you are free for the first 90 minutes, that start time counts you out.

**2. Free counts 1, Maybe counts half.** If you painted the whole stretch **Free**, you add 1 to that time's score. If any of it is **Maybe** (and the rest **Free**), you add a half.

**3. Not enough people, no suggestion.** Times where fewer people can come than the plan's **Minimum people** are left out. Both Free and Maybe count toward the minimum.

**4. Highest score wins, earliest breaks ties.** When two times score the same, the earlier one ranks first.

## A worked example

The screenshot above is a 2-hour dinner in a group of six, painted on a 30-minute grid.

| Start | Free | Maybe | Score |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| Fri, Nov 13, 6:00 PM | 4 | 0 | 4 |
| Sun, Nov 15, 5:00 PM | 3 | 1 | 3.5 |
| Fri, Nov 13, 6:30 PM | 3 | 0 | 3 |
| Fri, Nov 13, 7:00 PM | 3 | 0 | 3 |
| Sat, Nov 14, 6:00 PM | 2 | 1 | 2.5 |

Friday at 6:00 wins with four people free from 6:00 to 8:00. Sunday comes second: one fewer person is fully free, but a Maybe adds half a point. Friday at 6:30 and 7:00 tie at 3, so the earlier one is listed first.

Each suggestion says who would miss out, like **Without Kai and Lena**, or **Everyone can make it**.

## Why is a time missing?

- **Someone's painting stops short.** For a 2-hour event, a 7 PM start needs people free until 9 PM. Painting until 8:30 does not count.
- **It falls under the minimum.** Fewer people can make it than the plan's **Minimum people**. Nudge the people who have not answered yet.
- **Nobody painted it.** Potluck only suggests start times that someone painted.

If no time works yet, the card says **No time works for enough people yet. Nudge the stragglers or widen the window.**

:::tip[See it on the grid]
Hover over or focus a suggestion to light up its cells on the group heatmap. Handy for spotting near misses: a time where one person is 30 minutes short.
:::
