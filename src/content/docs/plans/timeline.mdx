The timeline is your upcoming plans from every group in one list, sorted by day. It appears in two places:

- **Coming up** on Home shows the next few. Tap **See all** for the rest.
- **Plans** shows the full list, with polls and past plans too. Filter by group at the top.

## Reading a row

Each row shows the date, the plan's emoji and title, the time and place, and which group it belongs to. Days are labeled **Today**, **Tomorrow**, the day of the week for the next few days, and the date after that.

The tag on the right says what the plan needs:

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Add your times** | The poll is open and you have not painted yet. See [Painting your availability](/plans/availability/). |
| **Picking a time** | The poll is open and you have answered. Waiting on the others. |
| **RSVP** | The time is locked in and you have not said if you are coming. See [RSVP](/plans/lock-in/#rsvp). |
| **going** count | You have answered. Shows how many people are going. |
| **Called off** | The plan was cancelled. |

Plans that need you also appear under **Needs you** on Home, so you rarely have to go looking.

:::note[Good to know]
Times show in your time zone. If you travel, change it on the [Me page](/account/#preferences) and every plan shifts to match.
:::

An empty timeline says **Nothing on the books**. Tap **Plan something** to fix that.
