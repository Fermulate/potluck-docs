## Your balance

The card at the top is your total in this group: **You owe**, **You are owed**, or **You are all square**. It counts every expense and payment in the group. **Settle up** starts a payment; see [Recording a payment](/balances/settle-up/).

## Who pays whom

This list says exactly which payments would square the group. Your own rows come first. Rows where you owe have a **Settle up** button, and rows where someone owes you have a [Remind](/balances/settle-up/#remind-someone) button (or [Record payment](/balances/settle-up/#someone-not-on-potluck-yet-paid) for someone not on Potluck yet). If you have more than one row, the card at the top also offers [Settle everything](/balances/settle-up/#settle-everything-at-once).

Below it, **Everyone** shows each member's total: **is owed**, **owes**, or **settled up**.

## Simplified debts

Groups simplify debts unless an owner or admin turns it off in [Group settings](/groups/settings/#house-rules). The setting's description sums it up: **Fewer payments, same totals. Nobody pays more than they owe.**

### An example

Three friends, two expenses:

- Ava paid $60 for groceries for herself and Ben. Ben owes Ava $30.
- Ben paid $60 for concert tickets for himself and Cam. Cam owes Ben $30.

Without simplifying, that is two payments: Ben pays Ava $30, and Cam pays Ben $30. Ben's money just passes through him.

With simplifying, Potluck looks at where everyone ends up:

| | Paid | Their share | Net |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| Ava | $60 | $30 | is owed $30 |
| Ben | $60 | $60 | settled up |
| Cam | $0 | $30 | owes $30 |

So the list shows one payment: **Cam owes Ava $30**. Everyone ends up in the same place, with one payment instead of two.

### How it decides

1. It works out each person's net balance: what they paid, minus their share of everything, adjusted for payments already made.
2. It takes the person who is owed the most and the person who owes the most, and has the second pay the first as much as they can.
3. It repeats until everyone is at zero.

A group of five never needs more than four payments this way.

:::note[Good to know]
Simplifying can ask you to pay someone you never split anything with directly, like Cam paying Ava above. Your total stays the same. If your group prefers each pair to settle on its own, turn off **Simplify debts**. The list is then called **Every debt**, and each pair's debts are netted only against each other.
:::

## Balances across groups

Each group has its own balances. Home adds them up for you under **Your balance**, and the [Friends](/friends/) page shows your one-on-one tabs. Money owed in one group is never moved into another.
