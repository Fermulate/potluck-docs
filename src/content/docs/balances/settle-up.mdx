Potluck does not move money. You pay people the way you already do (cash, a bank transfer, a payment app) and then record it in Potluck so the balance goes down.

## Settle up

<Steps>

1. On the group's **Balances** tab, tap **Settle up**. You can also tap the big plus button, then **Settle up**, and choose the group under **Which group are you settling?**

2. Under **Who are you paying?**, pick the person. People you owe are listed first under **Suggested**, with the amount. Anyone else in the group is under **Everyone else**.

   <Screenshot
     name="settle"
     alt="The Settle up screen asking Who are you paying? with the hint Payments go from you to them. Under Suggested, Maya Ruiz with You owe Maya $70.22. Under Everyone else, Jo Lindqvist, Kai Moana, Lena Weber and Noor Haddad."
   />

3. Check the **Amount**. It starts at what you owe that person, and you can pay less (a partial payment) or a different amount.

4. Add a **Note** if you like, such as how you paid.

5. Tap **Record payment**.

</Steps>

If that clears it, you see **All square with** their name and the balance ticks down to zero. If you paid part of it, Potluck says **Payment recorded** and how much you **still owe**. Tap **Back to** the group to return.

The person you paid gets a **You got paid** notification, and the payment shows in the group's expense list, for example **Noor paid you $35.10**.

:::note[Good to know]
- Payments always go **from you** to the other person. If someone paid you, they record it on their side.
- Payments are in the group's currency.
- In a [one-on-one tab](/friends/), tap **Settle up** on its **Balances** tab the same way.
:::

## Recorded a payment by mistake?

Find the payment in the group's **Expenses** tab, tap the **…** menu on its row and choose **Delete payment**. Confirm, and balances go back to how they were.

You can delete a payment if you made it, received it or recorded it. Group owners and admins can delete any payment. There is no undo, so check the row first.
