## Profile

- **Change photo** to upload a new picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB). Potluck crops it square.
- **Display name** is how friends see you on shared tabs and plans. Edit it and tap **Save**.
- **Email** is the address you sign in with.

## Preferences

**Time zone**
: Every time in Potluck shows in this zone. If your device is somewhere else (say you are traveling), a **Use my current time zone** button appears.

**Default currency**
: New groups you create start in this currency, and the balance on Home adds up groups in it. Existing groups keep their own currency. See [Currencies](/expenses/currencies/).

**Appearance**
: **System** follows your device's light or dark setting. **Light** and **Dark** pick one. This is saved on each device separately.

Changes save as soon as you make them.

## Notifications

**Open inbox** takes you to your notifications. **Push notifications** turns pings on or off for this device. See [Notifications](/notifications/).

## Calendar feed

Your calendar feed is a private link that lists every plan from all your groups that has a time locked in. Calendar apps check it regularly, so new plans, changes and called-off plans show up on their own.

<Steps>

1. On **Me**, find **Calendar**, then **Calendar feed**.
2. Tap **Subscribe** to open your calendar app and add it. Or tap **Copy feed link** and paste it into your calendar app's "add calendar from URL" option (in Google Calendar, that is **Other calendars**, then **From URL**).

</Steps>

Calendar apps refresh subscribed calendars on their own schedule, from a few minutes to several hours, so a new plan may take a while to appear.

### Reset the link

Anyone with your feed link can see your plans' titles, times, places and group names. If you shared it by accident, tap **Reset link** and confirm. **The old link stops working**, so subscribe again with the new one.

:::note[Good to know]
**Connect a calendar**, which would mark the times you are already busy on the availability grid, is marked **Coming soon**.
:::

## Sign out

Tap **Sign out** at the bottom of **Me**. You are signed out on this device only.

## Delete your account

<Steps>

1. At the bottom of **Me**, tap **Delete account**.
2. Read what will happen, then type **DELETE** in the box.
3. Tap **Delete my account**.

</Steps>

:::danger[This cannot be undone]
You are signed out and cannot sign back in to this account.
:::

### What happens to shared tabs

Your friends' money should not change because you left, so Potluck keeps the shared records and removes you from them:

- Your **name, photo and email** are removed. In every group you were in, you show as **Former member**.
- **Expenses and payments** you were part of stay, so everyone's totals still add up.
- **Open balances stay.** If you owed someone, the group still shows that debt against **Former member**. Settle up first so nobody is left waiting. See [Recording a payment](/balances/settle-up/).
- **Groups you own** pass to the longest-standing admin, or the longest-standing member if there is no admin. Groups with nobody else in them are deleted.
- Your availability, RSVPs, notifications, push settings, friends list and calendar feed are deleted. Invite links you made stop working.

See [Privacy and security](/privacy-and-security/) for what Potluck stores in the first place.
