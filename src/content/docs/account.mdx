## Profile

- **Change photo** to upload a new picture (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB). Potluck crops it square.
- **Display name** is how friends see you on shared tabs and plans. Edit it and tap **Save**.
- **Email** is the address you sign in with. To change it, see [Change your email](#change-your-email).

## Preferences

**Language**
: Pick **English**, **Español**, **Français**, **Deutsch** or **Português**. The app switches right away, no reload needed. Your choice is saved to your account, so it follows you to your other devices and is still there after you sign out and back in. Anything not translated yet shows in English.

**Time zone**
: Every time in Potluck shows in this zone. If your device is somewhere else (say you are traveling), a **Use my current time zone** button appears.

**Default currency**
: New groups you create start in this currency, and the balance on Home adds up groups in it. Existing groups keep their own currency. See [Currencies](/expenses/currencies/).

**Appearance**
: **System** follows your device's light or dark setting. **Light** and **Dark** pick one. This is saved on each device separately.

Changes save as soon as you make them.

## Payment methods

Save how you like to be paid, and people who owe you get a **Pay with** button for your app when they [settle up](/balances/settle-up/#pay-with-an-app). Potluck never moves money: the button only opens the app with your handle (and usually the amount) filled in.

<Steps>

1. On **Me**, under **Payment methods**, tap **Add a payment method**.
2. Under **Which app?**, pick one.
3. Type your handle, for example your **Venmo username** or **Cashtag**. Pasting your profile link works too, like `paypal.me/yourname`.
4. Tap **Save**.

</Steps>

You can save one of each:

| App | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| PayPal | Your PayPal.me username |
| Venmo | Your Venmo username |
| Cash App | Your Cashtag |
| Revolut | Your Revolut username |
| Monzo | Your Monzo.me username |
| UPI | Your UPI ID, like `name@bank` |
| Interac e-Transfer | The email or phone number you take e-Transfers on |
| Bank transfer | Your bank details, like an IBAN, or a sort code and account number |

If a handle does not look right for that app, Potluck says so before saving. Interac and bank details have no link: people see a **Copy** button instead.

**Change or remove one**
: Tap the pencil next to it to change it. Tap the bin to remove it. A message says it was removed, with an **Undo** button to bring it straight back.

### Who can see them

People you share a group or [one-on-one tab](/friends/#split-something-with-one-friend) with, and only them. A friend you share neither with does not see them, and nobody sees them while either of you has blocked the other. They see your payment methods when they settle up with you. When you delete your account, your payment methods are deleted too.

## Sign in and security

This part of **Me** holds your email, your password, two-step sign in and your devices.

### Change your email

<Steps>

1. Under **Sign in and security**, next to **Email**, tap **Change email**.
2. Type your **New email** and tap **Send confirmation links**.
3. Open the link we sent to your **old** address, and the one we sent to your **new** address. Either order works.

</Steps>

Nothing changes until both are confirmed. That way, someone who gets hold of your phone cannot quietly move your account to their inbox. Until then, **Me** says it is waiting for you to confirm from both inboxes. Once you open one link, Potluck reminds you to open the other.

**Cancel a pending change**
: Changed your mind, or made a typo? Tap **Cancel the change** under the waiting message. Both links stop working and your email stays as it was.

### Change your password

<Steps>

1. Under **Sign in and security**, next to **Password**, tap **Change password**.
2. Type your **Current password**.
3. Type your **New password** (at least 8 characters), then type it again.
4. Tap **Save password**.

</Steps>

If you signed in more than a day ago, Potluck also emails you a 6-digit code to check it is really you. Enter the **Code from the email** and tap **Save password** again.

Once it is saved, your other devices are signed out. The one you are using stays signed in.

**Forgot your password?**
: Tap **Forgot it?** next to **Current password**. It opens **Reset your password** with your email filled in. Tap **Send reset link** and pick a new password from the email. This also works if you have only ever signed in with emailed sign-in links and never had a password.

**Set a password**
: If you only ever signed in with Google or Apple, the button says **Set a password** instead. There is no current password to type, so just pick a new one. After that, you can sign in with your email and password too.

### Sign out everywhere

Lost a phone, or signed in on a shared computer? Next to **Devices**, tap **Sign out everywhere** and confirm. Every phone, tablet and browser on your account has to sign in again, including the one you are using.

## Two-step sign in

Two-step sign in adds a second check. After your password, sign-in link, or Google or Apple, Potluck asks for a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone. Someone who learns your password still cannot get in without it.

### Set it up

You need an authenticator app, like 1Password, Google Authenticator or Authy.

<Steps>

1. Under **Sign in and security**, next to **Two-step sign in**, tap **Set up**.
2. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. Cannot scan it? Tap **Copy key** and type or paste the key into the app instead.
3. Enter the 6-digit code the app shows and tap **Turn on**.
4. Save your recovery codes (see below), then tap **I saved them**.

</Steps>

**Me** now shows **On**, the date you turned it on, and how many recovery codes you have left.

### Recovery codes

When you turn on two-step sign in, Potluck shows you ten recovery codes. They are your way back in if you lose your phone. Tap **Copy codes** or **Download**, and keep them somewhere safe, like a password manager.

:::caution[You only see them once]
The codes are shown when you set up, and never again. The window only closes when you tap **I saved them**.
:::

Each code works once. Running low, or worried someone saw them? Tap **New recovery codes** and confirm. Your old codes stop working right away, and you get a fresh set of ten to save.

### Sign in with a code

When you sign in, Potluck shows **One more step**. Open your authenticator app, enter the 6-digit code for Potluck, and tap **Verify**.

Signed in to the wrong account? Tap **Use another account** to go back to **Sign in**.

### Lost your phone

<Steps>

1. Sign in as usual. On **One more step**, tap **Use a recovery code**.
2. Enter one of the codes you saved and tap **Verify**.

</Steps>

You are in, and two-step sign in is now **off**, so you can set it up again on your new phone. Do that from **Me** right away. Your old recovery codes stop working.

You get five tries every 15 minutes. After that, wait 15 minutes and try again.

Potluck also sends you a notice, **Two-step sign in is off**, to your inbox and devices, and to your email once email notifications are on. If you did not use a recovery code, someone else has your password and one of your codes: [change your password](#change-your-password) now and set up two-step sign in again.

:::note[No phone and no codes?]
Potluck cannot let you in on its own. Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Fermulate/potluck/issues), but leave out your email and any codes. Someone will check it is really you before turning two-step sign in off by hand.
:::

### Turn it off

Next to **Two-step sign in**, tap **Turn off**. Enter a code from your authenticator app and tap **Turn off**. Asking for a fresh code means a borrowed, unlocked phone is not enough. Your recovery codes are deleted too.

## Notifications

**Open inbox** takes you to your notifications. **Push notifications** turns pings on or off for this device.

Below that, **What to hear about** lets you pick which kinds of updates ping you or, once email is on, land in your email. Your inbox always gets everything. See [What to hear about](/notifications/#what-to-hear-about).

## Calendar feed

Your calendar feed is a private link that lists every plan from all your groups that has a time locked in. Calendar apps check it regularly, so new plans, changes and called-off plans show up on their own.

<Steps>

1. On **Me**, find **Calendar**, then **Calendar feed**.
2. Tap **Subscribe** to open your calendar app and add it. Or tap **Copy feed link** and paste it into your calendar app's "add calendar from URL" option (in Google Calendar, that is **Other calendars**, then **From URL**).

</Steps>

Calendar apps refresh subscribed calendars on their own schedule, from a few minutes to several hours, so a new plan may take a while to appear.

### Reset the link

Anyone with your feed link can see your plans' titles, times, places and group names. If you shared it by accident, tap **Reset link** and confirm. **The old link stops working**, so subscribe again with the new one.

:::note[Good to know]
**Connect a calendar**, which would mark the times you are already busy on the availability grid, is marked **Coming soon**.
:::

## Blocked people

**Blocked people** on **Me** lists everyone you blocked. When the list is empty, it says **Nobody blocked. Lovely.**

To unblock someone, tap **Unblock** next to their name. Potluck says they are unblocked. They are not told when you block or unblock them, and while they are blocked, nothing they do notifies you.

Unblocking does not make you friends again. Add them back with your [friend link](/friends/#your-friend-link), or from a group you share. Any invite email that was held back because of the block goes out now, unless another block still stands in the way, the group is archived, or the person who sent it has left the group. See [Block and report](/friends/#block-and-report).

To block someone, open their page from **Friends** while they are still your friend. See [Block someone](/friends/#block-someone).

## Export your data

Everything you put in Potluck is yours. **Your data** on **Me** has three downloads. Each one is made fresh when you tap it, and Potluck says where the file went.

### Download a backup

Under **Backup**, tap **Download a backup**. You get one JSON file with:

- your profile: name, sign-in email, photo link, time zone, default currency and language,
- your own [payment methods](#payment-methods), and nobody else's,
- your friends list,
- every group you are in, archived ones and your [one-on-one tabs](/friends/#split-something-with-one-friend) too, with its members and roles,
- every expense, with who paid, each share, line items and comments,
- every payment, with its comments,
- repeating expenses,
- plans, with RSVPs, availability and comments,
- the names of everyone the file mentions, including [people not on Potluck yet](/groups/#people-not-on-potluck-yet) and people who left.

Deleted expenses and payments are in it too, marked as deleted, since you can still restore them. Deleted comments are not. Receipt photos are not in the file, only a note of where each one is stored. For what is left out and how to keep the file safe, see [What your backup contains](/privacy-and-security/#what-your-backup-contains).

The backup is for keeping. The [import screen](/groups/settings/#import-expenses) reads spreadsheets, not backups.

### Every group as a spreadsheet

Under **Every group as a spreadsheet**, tap **Export everything**. You get one CSV file with every group you are in, your one-on-one tabs included, one after another. It uses the same layout as a [group's spreadsheet](/groups/#download-a-groups-expenses): every expense and payment, who paid, each person's share and balance, and a **Balance** row closing each group.

### What you share with one person

Under **What you share with one person**, pick a friend and tap **Export**. You get every expense and payment between the two of you, from your one-on-one tab and every group you share.

This file keeps only your columns and theirs. Everyone else is still named in **Paid by**, so each row makes sense. Instead of balance columns, it has **Between you and** their name: what each row did between you two. Positive means they owe you. Each group's total is what the two of you owe each other there.

This file leaves the others out, so it cannot be imported into a group. Export the whole group for that.

:::tip[Moving from Splitwise?]
Open the Potluck group the expenses belong in, then **Settings**, **Import**. See [Import expenses](/groups/settings/#import-expenses).
:::

## Sign out

Tap **Sign out** at the bottom of **Me**. You are signed out on this device only. To sign out on all of them, see [Sign out everywhere](#sign-out-everywhere).

## Delete your account

<Steps>

1. At the bottom of **Me**, tap **Delete account**.
2. Read what will happen, then type **DELETE** in the box.
3. Tap **Delete my account**.

</Steps>

:::danger[This cannot be undone]
You are signed out and cannot sign back in to this account.
:::

### What happens to shared tabs

Your friends' money should not change because you left, so Potluck keeps the shared records and removes you from them:

- Your **name, photo and email** are removed. In every group you were in, you show as **Former member**.
- **Expenses and payments** you were part of stay, so everyone's totals still add up.
- **Open balances stay.** If you owed someone, the group still shows that debt against **Former member**. Settle up first so nobody is left waiting. See [Recording a payment](/balances/settle-up/).
- **Groups you own** pass to the longest-standing admin, or the longest-standing member if there is no admin. A saved seat never takes over. Groups with nobody else in them who has an account are deleted.
- Your availability, RSVPs, notifications, push and email notification settings, friends list, payment methods and calendar feed are deleted. Invite links you made stop working.
- People you blocked are unblocked, without sending any invite the block held back. Seats you [saved for people not on Potluck yet](/groups/#people-not-on-potluck-yet) stay in groups that remain, and their claim links keep working.

See [Privacy and security](/privacy-and-security/) for what Potluck stores in the first place.
