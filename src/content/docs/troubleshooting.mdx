---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Fixes for sign-in links, invites, uploads, push notifications and other things that can go wrong.
---

Start with the message Potluck shows you. Most of them say what to do next. If yours is not here, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Fermulate/potluck/issues).

## Signing in

### The sign-in link says it has expired

**That link has expired or was already used.** Sign-in links work once and only for a short time. Go back to **Sign in** and ask for a new one. Open it on the same device and browser you asked from.

### The email never arrived

Check your spam folder, then tap **Send it again**. Make sure the address is right; **Use a different email** lets you fix it.

### "Confirm your email first"

Your account needs the confirmation link Potluck sent when you signed up. Find that email and open the link, then sign in again.

### I forgot my password

On **Sign in**, tap **Forgot password?**, enter your email and tap **Send reset link**. If the reset link has expired, ask for a new one.

### "Your session timed out"

You were signed out for safety. Sign in again and you will be back where you were.

## Groups and invites

### The invite link does not work

The page says **This invite has gone cold** and gives the reason: expired, used up, revoked, or not found. Ask someone in the group for a fresh link. See [Inviting people](/groups/invites/#when-a-link-does-not-work).

### Where are the link settings?

In the **Invite people** sheet, tap **Link settings** below your current link. Only the group owner sees **Joins as**. If the sheet says **Only owners and admins can invite people to this group.**, the group has turned off member invites.

### I cannot leave a group, or remove someone

Balances must be zero first. [Settle up](/balances/settle-up/), then try again. If you own the group, [transfer ownership](/groups/roles/#transfer-ownership) first.

### A group disappeared from my list

It may be archived. On **Groups**, tap **Show archived**. If it is not there, someone removed you or the owner deleted it.

## Expenses

### I cannot save an expense

Read the message under the form:

- **The split needs to add up to the total.** In **Exact**, the amounts must match the total exactly.
- **Percentages need to add up to 100%.** Drag the bar or tap **Even it out**.
- **Pick at least one person to split with.**
- **No exchange rate for that currency yet.** Pick another currency, or convert it yourself. See [Currencies](/expenses/currencies/).

### I cannot edit or delete an expense

Only the person who added it, anyone who paid for it, and the group's owner and admins can change it. Ask one of them. In an [archived group](/expenses/editing/#archived-groups-are-read-only), nobody can until it is unarchived. See [Editing and deleting](/expenses/editing/).

### A receipt will not upload

- **That file is over 10 MB.** Potluck shrinks most photos before uploading, so this is usually a big PDF or a HEIC photo outside Safari. Take a screenshot of it, or save it as a smaller file.
- **Use a photo or a PDF.** Only PNG, JPEG, WebP and HEIC photos and PDFs are accepted.
- **Five is the limit. Remove one to add another.** An expense holds up to five files.
- **The receipt did not upload. Try again.** The expense was not saved. Tap save again: files that already went up are not sent twice.
- **Saved, but the files did not attach. Edit the expense to add them again.** The changes to the expense were saved, and it kept the files it had before. Open it, tap **Edit** and add them again.
- **The files on this expense did not load, so they cannot be changed yet.** Tap **Try again**. Until they load, the files on the expense cannot be added to or removed, so a save never drops one you did not see.

### A receipt will not scan

- **The first scan needs a connection to fetch the reader. Try again online.** The first scan downloads the reader once. After that, scanning works offline.
- **That one did not read. Try a sharper photo, flat and well lit.** Lay the receipt flat in good light and fill the photo with it.
- **Nothing on it looked like prices.** Tap **Just attach the photo** and fill in the expense by hand.
- **The items do not add up.** Check each price against the photo on **Here is what it says**, or untick the items to use the total only.
- **There is no Scan a receipt button.** It is not there while editing a repeating expense, or on an expense that names someone who has left the group.

See [Getting a good read](/expenses/receipts/#getting-a-good-read).

### My balance looks wrong

Open the group's **Expenses** tab and check each line's **you lent** or **you borrowed**. Common causes: the wrong person under **Paid by**, someone left out of the split, or a payment recorded twice. If simplify debts is on, the list of who pays whom may look different from your own expenses but totals the same. See [Balances](/balances/#simplified-debts).

## Plans

### My times did not save

Under the grid, Potluck says **Your times did not save. We will keep trying.** It retries on its own when you are back online. If it gives up, tap **Try again**.

### The grid scrolls instead of painting

On a phone, start your drag inside the grid. Dragging from outside scrolls the page. See [Painting your availability](/plans/availability/#mouse-touch-and-keyboard).

### The time I wanted is not in Best times

Everyone counted has to be free for the whole length of the plan, and the time has to reach the plan's minimum. See [Why is a time missing?](/plans/best-time/#why-is-a-time-missing).

### Times look off by a few hours

Potluck shows times in the zone set on **Me**. Check **Time zone** there, or tap **Use my current time zone**.

## Notifications

### Push notifications do not arrive

- Check that **Push notifications** is on under **Me** for this device. It says **Push is on for this device.**
- Check that the row for that kind of update has its **Push** switch on under [What to hear about](/notifications/#what-to-hear-about) on **Me**. These switches follow your account, so turning one off on any device stops that ping everywhere.
- If it says **Notifications are blocked in your browser settings.**, allow notifications for Potluck in your browser or phone settings, then turn push on again.
- On iPhone and iPad, Potluck must be [added to your home screen](/getting-started/#iphone-and-ipad) and opened from there.
- If it says **This browser cannot do push notifications**, try a current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
- If it says **Push is not set up on this server yet**, push is not available on this Potluck server. The inbox still has everything.

### The calendar feed is not updating

Calendar apps refresh subscribed feeds on their own schedule, sometimes only every few hours. If it never updates, check that you did not [reset the link](/account/#reset-the-link) since subscribing.

## Everything else

### "This version is out of date"

Refresh the page, or close and reopen the app, to load the latest version.

### "Easy there. Try again in a moment."

Too many requests in a short time. Wait a few seconds and try again.

### "Something burned in the kitchen"

An unexpected error. Try again. If it keeps happening, open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Fermulate/potluck/issues) and say what you were doing.
