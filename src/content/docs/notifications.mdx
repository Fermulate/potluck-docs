Potluck keeps you posted in two ways. The **inbox** inside the app always has every update. **Push notifications** are optional pings on your phone or computer.

## The inbox

On a computer, click **Notifications** in the left rail. On a phone, tap **Me**, then **Open inbox**. The red badge on **Me** (or next to **Notifications**) counts what you have not read.

New updates are under **New**, older ones under **Earlier**. Tap one to go straight to the expense, plan or group it is about. Tap **Mark all as read** to clear the badge. When there is nothing left, the inbox says **All caught up**.

## What you hear about

| Notification | When | Who gets it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New expense in** a group | Someone adds an expense | The people in the split and the payer |
| **Expense updated** | Someone changes an expense | The people in it |
| **You got paid** | Someone records a payment to you | The person paid |
| **New face in** a group | Someone joins | The owner and admins |
| **Something is cooking** | Someone starts a plan | The group |
| **When are you free?** | Someone nudges you to add your times | The people nudged |
| **It's a date** | A plan is locked in | The group |
| **Plans changed** | A plan is called off | The group |
| **Coming up** | About a day before a plan | People going or maybe |
| **Are you in?** | About a day before a plan | People who have not answered |
| **New comment** | Someone comments | People in that expense, or the organizer and people coming to that plan |

You never get notified about your own actions.

## Turn on push notifications

<Steps>

1. Go to **Me** and find **Notifications**.
2. Turn on **Push notifications**.
3. When your browser asks for permission, tap **Allow**.

</Steps>

When it works, the switch says **Push is on for this device.** Push is set per device: turn it on separately on your phone and your laptop.

:::tip[On iPhone and iPad]
Add Potluck to your home screen first and open it from there, then turn on push. Safari in a normal tab cannot show notifications. See [Install Potluck as an app](/getting-started/#install-potluck-as-an-app).
:::

## Turn push off

Turn off **Push notifications** on **Me**. That device stops getting pings right away. The inbox keeps working, as the app says: **You will always see updates in your inbox.**

You can also block notifications for Potluck in your browser or phone settings. Potluck then shows **Notifications are blocked in your browser settings.** To turn push back on later, allow notifications for the site in those settings first.

:::note[Good to know]
Push is all or nothing for now. You cannot pick which kinds of notifications to get pushed. The inbox always gets all of them.
:::

If push does not arrive, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/#push-notifications-do-not-arrive).
