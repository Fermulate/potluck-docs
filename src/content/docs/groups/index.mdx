A group is a shared tab: the roommates, a trip, the Thursday dinner crew. Everything you split and everything you plan lives in a group.

## Create a group

<Steps>

1. Open **Groups** and tap **New group**. (Home and the first-run screen have the same button.)

2. Give it a **Group name**, pick an **Emoji** (or type your own in **Or type your own**) and a **Color**.

3. Choose the **Currency**. Balances in this group are kept in it, and it locks once the first expense or payment is added. People can still add expenses in other currencies; see [Currencies](/expenses/currencies/).

4. Got friends who are not on Potluck yet? Type each name under **Anyone not on Potluck yet?** and tap **Add**. They are in the group as soon as it opens. See [People not on Potluck yet](#people-not-on-potluck-yet).

5. Tap **Create group**.

</Steps>

You become the group's **Owner**. Potluck opens the **Members** tab with a ready-to-go invite card, because a table for one is no fun.

Tap **Create link** and send it to your friends. [Inviting people](/groups/invites/) covers the options.

## Inside a group

<Screenshot
  name="group-expenses"
  alt="The Supper club group. The header shows the group emoji, six member avatars and the line You are owed $282.44 next to an Invite button. Below are the tabs Expenses, Balances, Plans and Members, a Search expenses field with a download button, and today's expenses and payments."

The header shows the group's emoji, who is in it and your balance in plain words: **You are owed**, **You owe**, or **All square here**. The **Invite** button is always one tap away.

The tabs underneath:

| Tab | What is there |
| --- | --- |
| **Expenses** | Every expense and payment, newest first. Search, [filter](/expenses/#filter-the-list) by person, date, category or type, or download a CSV. [Repeating expenses](/expenses/recurring/) sit at the top. |
| **Balances** | Who owes whom, and the **Settle up** button. See [Balances](/balances/). |
| **Totals** | What the group spent and your share, by month and category, plus **Recently deleted**. See [Totals and charts](/expenses/search-and-totals/#totals-and-charts). |
| **Plans** | The group's open polls and what is on the calendar. See [Plans](/plans/). |
| **Members** | Everyone in the group, their role and their balance. See [Roles](/groups/roles/). |
| **Settings** | Name, emoji, color, currency, house rules, default split, archive, leave and delete. See [Group settings](/groups/settings/). |

On a phone the tabs scroll sideways, so swipe left to reach **Members** and **Settings**.

:::note[Good to know]
A one-on-one tab with a friend works like a small group with only **Expenses**, **Balances** and **Totals**. See [Friends](/friends/).
:::

## People not on Potluck yet

Not everyone signs up on day one. You can add someone by name and split with them right away. Potluck saves them a seat, and when they join, everything split for them becomes theirs.

In the group they show with a dashed outline and a **Not on Potluck yet** badge. The line under their name says where their invite goes, like **Invite goes to s\*\*\*@example.com**, or **No email yet: share their link**.

### Add someone by name

You can add someone from three places:

- **Members**: tap **Add someone** at the end of the list.
- **Adding an expense**: tap **Someone new** next to the people in the split. They go straight into the split.
- **New group**: type their names under **Anyone not on Potluck yet?** before you tap **Create group**. Add their emails later from **Members**.

<Steps>

1. Type their **Name**.

2. Add their **Email (optional)** if you know it. Potluck sends them one invite, and one reminder if you ask. Nothing else.

3. Tap **Add**. To add a few people in a row, tap **Add another** instead.

</Steps>

Potluck says **Sam is in. Split away.**, or **Their invite is on its way.** if you added an email.

Anyone who can [make invite links](/groups/invites/) in the group can add people this way. That is owners and admins, and regular members unless the group turned off **Members can invite people**.

### Split with them

Someone not on Potluck yet is a regular member for money. Put them in a split, pick them as the person who paid, and they get a balance like everyone else. It shows on **Balances** under their name.

They cannot sign in, so they cannot record what they paid you. Record it for them: see [Someone not on Potluck yet paid](/balances/settle-up/#someone-not-on-potluck-yet-paid).

They are left out of anything that needs an answer from them: plans, availability and RSVPs. They can only be a regular member, never an admin or the owner.

### Their claim link and reminder

Every saved seat has its own claim link. On **Members**, tap the three-dot button next to them:

- **Share their link** opens your share sheet with a note like "Sam, your seat in Supper club on Potluck is saved". On a computer it copies the link.
- **Send a reminder** sends their invite email again. Each seat gets **one** reminder. After that, the line under their name says when it went, like **Reminded Nov 13**.

Anyone in the group can share the link or send the reminder. The reminder needs an email on the seat.

:::caution[Send the link to them only]
A seat without an email can be taken by whoever opens its link and signs in. A seat with an email can only be taken by someone who signs in with that address.
:::

### When they join

They take their seat in one of two ways:

- **With their link or invite email.** The page says **A seat is saved for you**. They tap **Create an account to take it**, **Sign in to take it** or **Take my seat**. See [Take a saved seat](/groups/invites/#take-a-saved-seat).
- **Automatically.** When someone signs up and confirms the email saved on a seat, they get that seat without doing anything else. Their inbox says they were added to the group. Taking a seat with a link does not send this.

Either way, their email has to be confirmed first.

Everything moves over to their account: the expenses they are in, what they paid, payments, repeating expenses and the group's default split. **Balances stay exactly the same.** Only the name on them changes. They also show up on the [Friends](/friends/) list of everyone in the group.

Older lines in the group's activity keep the seat's name, so they still read "Ava added Sam". A new line says "Cleo took Sam's seat".

This works in [archived groups](#archived-groups) too. If they are already in the group under their own name, the seat cannot be taken: Potluck says **You are already in this group under your own name.**

### Someone already on Potluck

If the email you type already belongs to a Potluck account, Potluck adds that person directly, under their own name. You see **Sam is already on Potluck, so they are in.** and they get a **Welcome to** the group notification.

If they are already in the group, Potluck says **Someone with that email is already in this group.** If a seat in the group already has that email, it says **Someone with that email is already waiting to join.**

### Change their name or email

On **Members**, tap the three-dot button next to them, then **Edit name or email**.

- **Name**: whoever added them can rename them, and so can anyone who can add people to the group.
- **Email**: only whoever added them, or an owner or admin, can change it.

A new email gets a fresh invite, and **the old link stops working**. Potluck says **Saved. A fresh invite is on its way.** If someone else changed the email of a seat you added, you get a notification. Each seat's invite can go to three addresses at most.

### Remove a seat

On **Members**, tap the three-dot button next to them, then **Remove** and their name.

- Whoever added them can remove them, and so can owners and admins.
- Their balance has to be zero first. Settle up with them, or [record what they paid](/balances/settle-up/#someone-not-on-potluck-yet-paid), until it is.

Removing a seat closes its claim link, and nothing more is sent to its email. Expenses that name them keep their name.

:::note[Good to know]
- Adding the same person to two groups makes two seats. They can take both.
- A saved seat cannot take over a group. See [Transfer ownership](/groups/roles/#transfer-ownership) for what happens when you leave a group where nobody else has an account.
:::

## Download a group's expenses

On the **Expenses** tab, tap the download button next to the search field (**Export CSV**). You get a spreadsheet with the date, description, category, who paid, the amount and currency, the exchange rate, the amount in the group currency, notes, and each person's share. When several people paid, **Paid by** lists each of them with what they paid. Refunds show as negative amounts and shares.

## Archived groups

When a trip is over, archive the group from [Group settings](/groups/settings/#archive-a-group). It drops off your list but nothing is lost. On **Groups**, tap **Show archived** to find it again.
