Every group has exactly one **Owner**, any number of **Admins**, and everyone else is a **Member**. You can see each person's role on the **Members** tab.

## Who can do what

| | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Add expenses, and edit or delete the ones you added or paid | yes | yes | yes |
| Edit or delete anyone's expense | yes | yes | no |
| Change, skip, pause or stop a [repeating expense](/expenses/recurring/#who-can-change-it) | yes | yes | if they set it up, pay or are in the split |
| Record a payment you made or received, and edit, delete or restore payments you are in or recorded | yes | yes | yes |
| Record a payment between two other people | yes | yes | no |
| Edit, delete or restore anyone's payment | yes | yes | no |
| Add your times, RSVP and comment on plans | yes | yes | yes |
| Plan something and nudge people | yes | yes | yes |
| Lock in or call off a plan | yes | yes | only plans they organized |
| Make member invite links | yes | yes | if the group allows it |
| Make admin invite links | yes | no | no |
| Add [people not on Potluck yet](/groups/#people-not-on-potluck-yet) | yes | yes | if the group allows it |
| Change the email of someone not on Potluck yet | yes | yes | if they added them |
| Remove someone not on Potluck yet, once settled | yes | yes | if they added them |
| Change roles and remove people | yes | regular members only | no |
| Change group settings, archive the group | yes | yes | no |
| Change the group's default split | yes | yes | yes |
| Transfer ownership, delete the group | yes | no | no |

In the app's own words: the owner "runs the place", admins "can edit settings, manage members, and fix anyone's expenses", and members "add expenses, vote on plans, and settle up".

## Change someone's role

<Steps>

1. On the **Members** tab, tap the three-dot button next to the person.
2. Tap **Make admin** or **Make regular member**, then **Confirm**.

</Steps>

Owners can change anyone except themselves. Admins can only change regular members, which includes promoting a member to admin.

## Remove someone

<Steps>

1. On the **Members** tab, tap the three-dot button next to the person.
2. Tap **Remove from group**, then **Remove**.

</Steps>

Owners can remove anyone. Admins can remove regular members. Nobody can remove the owner.

## Leave a group

Tap **Leave group** at the bottom of the **Members** tab (it is also under **The exit** in [Group settings](/groups/settings/)), then confirm.

## The open balance rule

You cannot leave a group, or remove someone from it, while that person still owes money or is owed money there. Potluck says **You still have an open balance here. Settle up first.** or **still has an open balance. Settle up first.**

This keeps the books honest. If someone could walk away mid-tab, the rest of the group would be left holding a debt nobody can collect. [Settle up](/balances/settle-up/) until the balance is zero, then leave.

:::note[Good to know]
Leaving does not erase what you did. Expenses you added or were part of stay in the group, so everyone else's totals still add up.
:::

## Transfer ownership

The owner cannot leave until someone else owns the group. Potluck says **Hand ownership to someone else before you go.**

<Steps>

1. On the **Members** tab, tap the three-dot button next to the new owner.
2. Tap **Make owner**, then confirm.

</Steps>

They become the owner and you become an admin. From there you can leave like anyone else.

If nobody else in a group you own has an account, you can leave without handing it over. [Saved seats](/groups/#people-not-on-potluck-yet) cannot take over, so the group is deleted when you go, and Potluck asks first. Every seat's balance has to be zero before you can.
