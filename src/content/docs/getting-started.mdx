Potluck runs in your browser, on your phone and on your computer. There is nothing to download first, and it costs nothing.

## Create an account

<Steps>

1. Open [Potluck](https://potluck.fermulate.com) and tap **Get started**.

2. On **Join the table**, fill in **Your name**, **Email** and **Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters.

   <Screenshot
     name="sign-up"
     alt="The Join the table screen with fields for your name, email and password, and a red Create account button."
   />

3. Tap **Create account**. If Potluck asks you to confirm your email, open the link it sends you.

</Steps>

Already have an account? Tap **Sign in** instead. You can type your password, or tap **Email me a sign-in link** and sign in from your inbox without one.

<Screenshot
  name="sign-in"
  alt="The Welcome back screen with Email and Password fields, a Forgot password? link, a red Sign in button, an Email me a sign-in link button, and New here? Create an account."
/>

:::note[Good to know]
If someone sent you an invite link, open that link first. The invite page has its own **Create an account to join** button, and you land in the group as soon as you are signed up. See [Joining a group](/groups/invites/#join-with-a-link).
:::

## Set up your profile

The first time you sign in, Potluck asks two quick things.

<Steps>

1. **First, the basics.** Add a photo if you like (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB), check your **Display name**, and confirm your **Time zone** and **Default currency**. Potluck guesses the time zone from your device. New groups you create start in your default currency.

   <Screenshot
     name="welcome"
     alt="Step 1 of 2, First, the basics: a round avatar with initials, an Add a photo link, the display name, a time zone set to America / Toronto, a default currency of USD, and a Looks good button."
   />

2. Tap **Looks good**.

3. **Start a group.** Pick **Create a group**, or **I have an invite link** to paste a link someone sent you. **Maybe later** takes you straight to Home.

</Steps>

You can change all of this later on the [Me page](/account/).

## Install Potluck as an app

Potluck is a web app you can add to your home screen. It then opens full screen, without the browser bars, and it can send [push notifications](/notifications/).

### iPhone and iPad

<Steps>

1. Open Potluck in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

</Steps>

:::tip[On iPhone]
Push notifications only work after you add Potluck to your home screen and open it from there.
:::

### Android

<Steps>

1. Open Potluck in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**).

</Steps>

### Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then **Install**. Potluck gets its own window and a spot in your dock or Start menu.

On Android and on a computer, right-click or long-press the installed icon to jump straight to **Add expense** or **Plan something**.

## A tour of Home

Home is where Potluck tells you what needs you right now.

<Screenshot
  name="home"
  alt="Home on a phone. A greeting reads Afternoon, Noor. Below it, a dark balance card says All told, you owe $35.10, split into You owe $35.10 and Owed to you $0.00, with one group listed. Under that, a Needs you card asks about Dinner at mine with Can't and Going buttons, then a Coming up list. The tab bar at the bottom has Home, Groups, a big plus button, Plans and Me."
/>

- **The greeting** says how many things need you.
- **Your balance** adds up every group in your default currency: **All told, you owe** or **All told, you are owed**, with **You owe** and **Owed to you** underneath and one line per group. Tap a group to open it. Groups in other currencies show up as **Plus balances in** that currency.
- **Needs you** lists plans waiting on you: polls where you have not added your times yet, and plans where you have not said whether you are going.
- **Coming up** shows plans that are on the calendar. **See all** opens [Plans](/plans/).
- **Your groups** lists your groups, with a shortcut to a **New group**.

### Getting around

On a phone, the tab bar at the bottom has **Home**, **Groups**, **Plans** and **Me**. The big plus button in the middle opens **What are we doing?** with three shortcuts: **Add expense**, **Plan something** and **Settle up**.
