Potluck runs in your browser, on your phone and on your computer. There is nothing to download first, and it costs nothing.

No account yet? Start with [Create an account](#create-an-account), right below. Already signed in? These are the three steps from the **Set the table** card on Home, each with the page that walks you through it:

<FirstSteps />

## Create an account

<Steps>

1. Open [Potluck](https://potluck.fermulate.com) and tap **Get started**.

2. On **Join the table**, pick one way to sign up:

   - **Continue with Google** at the top. Choose your Google account on Google's page, and you come back to Potluck signed in. There is no confirmation email to open, because Google has already confirmed your address.
   - Or fill in **Your name**, **Email** and **Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters.

   <Screenshot
     name="sign-up"
     alt="The Join the table screen with a Continue with Google button at the top, then or, then Your name, Email and Password fields with the hint At least 8 characters, a Create account button, Already have an account? Sign in, and the line What Potluck keeps about you, and who sees it: with a Privacy and security link."
   />

3. If you used the form, tap **Create account**. Potluck says **Check your inbox**: open the link it sends you to confirm your email. You cannot sign in with your password until you do.

</Steps>

Already have a Potluck account on the same email as your Google account? **Continue with Google** opens that account instead of making a second one.

Wondering what happens to your details? Under the form on both **Join the table** and **Welcome back**, **What Potluck keeps about you, and who sees it:** is followed by a [Privacy and security](/privacy-and-security/) link. The footer of Potluck's front page has a **Privacy** link to the same page.

Already have an account? Tap **Sign in** instead. **Continue with Google** works here too, and makes your account if you do not have one yet. You can also type your password, or tap **Email me a sign-in link** and sign in from your inbox without one.

## Set up your profile

The first time you sign in, Potluck asks two quick things. This happens however you signed up, Google included.

<Steps>

1. **First, the basics.** Add a photo if you like (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF, up to 5 MB), check your **Display name**, and confirm your **Time zone** and **Default currency**. Potluck guesses the time zone from your device. New groups you create start in your default currency.

   If you signed up with Google, your display name starts as the name on your Google account. Your Google photo is not copied over, so add one here if you want one.

   <Screenshot
     name="welcome"
     alt="Step 1 of 2, First, the basics: a round avatar with initials, an Add a photo link, the display name, a time zone set to America / Toronto, a default currency of USD, and a Looks good button."
   />

2. Tap **Looks good**.

3. **Start a group.** Pick **Create a group**, or **I have an invite link** to paste a link someone sent you. **Maybe later** takes you straight to Home.

</Steps>

You can change all of this later on the [Me page](/account/).

## Install Potluck as an app

Potluck is a web app you can add to your home screen. It then opens full screen, without the browser bars, and it can send [push notifications](/notifications/).

### iPhone and iPad

<Steps>

1. Open Potluck in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

</Steps>

:::tip[On iPhone]
Push notifications only work after you add Potluck to your home screen and open it from there.
:::

### Android

<Steps>

1. Open Potluck in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the three-dot menu.
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**).

</Steps>

### Computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then **Install**. Potluck gets its own window and a spot in your dock or Start menu.

On Android and on a computer, right-click or long-press the installed icon to jump straight to **Add expense** or **Plan something**.

## A tour of Home

Home is where Potluck tells you what needs you right now.

Under that, a Needs you card asks about Dinner at mine with Can't and Going buttons, then a Coming up list. The tab bar at the bottom has Home, Groups, a big plus button, Plans and Me."
/>

- **The greeting** says how many things need you. With no plans waiting but money still owed either way, in any currency, it says **No plans waiting on you. Your balances are below.** It only says **Nothing needs you right now.** when there is neither.
- **Your balance** says what it counts the way **Groups** and **Friends** list them: **across 5 groups** when you have no one-on-one tabs, **with 2 friends** when you are in no other group, or **across your groups and friends** when you have both. It adds up every group in your default currency: **All told, you owe** or **All told, you are owed**, with **You owe** and **Owed to you** underneath and one line per group. A zero amount is grey, while **All square** stays green. Tap a group to open it. A one-on-one tab is listed by your friend's name. Totals in other currencies are listed underneath, each one as it is and never converted, like **Plus you are owed €12.00 and you owe £5.00**.
- **Needs you** lists plans waiting on you: polls where you have not added your times yet, and plans where you have not said whether you are going.
- **Coming up** shows plans that are on the calendar. **See all** opens [Plans](/plans/).
- **Your groups** lists your groups, with a shortcut to a **New group**.

Not in a group yet? While your first steps card is showing, Home leaves out **Coming up** and **Your groups**, because both need a group. Hide the card and they come back.

### Your first steps

When you are new, Home shows a card called **Set the table** until one of your groups has someone else in it. It has three steps and keeps count, like **1 of 3 done**:

1. **Start a group.** Tap **Create a group**. See [Create a group](/groups/#create-a-group).
2. **Add your people.** Once you have a group, tap **Add people**. It opens the group's **Members** tab, where you can send an invite link or add someone by name. See [Inviting people](/groups/invites/).
3. **Add the first expense.** See [Adding an expense](/expenses/).

Only the step you are on has a button. **Getting started** at the bottom of the card opens this page.

The card goes away by itself as soon as one of your groups has more than one member. It is for newcomers only, so you never see it if you have a one-on-one tab with a friend or an archived group. To put it away sooner, tap the X in its corner (**Hide these steps**). That hides it in the browser you are using, so another phone or computer still shows it.
